THE WEIGHT OF REDEMPTION

Seeking redemption after accidentally causing a runner’s death, a 200 kg stadium custodian assumes the deceased athlete’s identity and follows his handwritten training diary to run a full marathon. Along the 42.2 km journey, he discovers that the heaviest weight he must shed is not his flesh, but decades of childhood trauma, fear, and lies.

05 · NARRATIVE SYNOPSIS BY ACTS DRAMATURGY

ANATOMY OF REDEMPTION

ACT I · THE INCITING TRAGEDY

10 METERS OF ETERNITY

Illiterate 207 kg custodian Rasul witnesses runner Artem collapse on the night track. Tripping on untied laces, Rasul accidentally crushes Artem’s inhaler. Panicked, he takes Artem’s backpack, SOS watch, and diary.

ACT II (PART 1) · THE "EXTRAS" CLINIC

THE BRASS SCALES & FIRST LETTERS

Entering the JANA MEN center in Astana as "Sultan", Rasul faces mentor Aitym Zhakupov’s brass scales and learns from Elvira how to double-knot his laces and write his own name.

ACT II (PART 2) · DARK NIGHT OF THE SOUL

COLLAPSE OF THE MYTH

Humiliated on a livestream by influencer Dinara and confronted by Artem’s daughter Yulia—who exposes her father’s diary as a fabrication—Rasul leaps from the clinic roof in Artem’s blue jacket to run the marathon.

ACT III & EPILOGUE · THE MARATHON

THE ULTIMATE VICTORY

10 meters from the finish line, Rasul conquers the phantom of his abusive stepfather, turns his back on the finish arch, and walks backward to save Yulia from an asthma attack with a fresh inhaler.

06 · DOCUMENTARY AUTHENTICITY & TRANSMEDIA BREAKING THE 4TH WALL

BEYOND FICTION: THE JANA MEN PHENOMENON

Founded by General Producer Aitym Zhakupov, the real-life IBA JANA MEN movement has helped participants shed over 1,500+ kg of critical weight while overcoming deep psychological trauma.

In NOT EXTRA, real alumni of JANA MEN appear alongside professional actors, and the climax was captured inside a live street race in Astana—fusing scripted cinema with visceral documentary truth.
